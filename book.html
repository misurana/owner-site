Anthology Overview

Kuchh Bhi (कुछ भी) is a raw, soulful digital compilation of original Hindi poetry and prose written and curated by Mukesh Rana under the literary pen name Misu. It maps the silent conflicts, school nostalgia, heartbreak, and quiet resilience of the human spirit. Using elegant Devnagari scripts and romanized phonetics, the anthology invites readers to explore emotional and rhythmic storytelling.
